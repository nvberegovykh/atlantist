The Two-Fee Model

1

Itemized Stripe Fee

We pass Stripe's processing fee (2.9% + $0.30 per transaction) to the buyer as a visible line item. This is Stripe's actual cost — Atlantist keeps zero of it.

2

2% Platform Commission

Atlantist retains exactly 2% of the listing price as our platform fee. This funds platform maintenance, hosting, and continued development. No variable markups, no hidden margins.

3

Creator Receives 98%

The creator receives the listing price minus the 2% platform commission. Payouts are automated via Stripe Connect — creators get paid directly to their Stripe account.

Smart Pricing for Low-Cost Assets

For assets priced under $5, the fixed $0.30 Stripe fee can erode creator margins. Our smart pricing tier uses a self-correcting formula that ensures creators always earn a minimum meaningful amount even on low-price items. The adjustment is automatic — creators don't need to set it manually.

Common Questions

Who pays the Stripe processing fee?
The buyer pays it — it's shown as a separate line item at checkout. Creators never absorb Stripe's fees. This keeps creator payouts clean and predictable.
How do creators get paid?
Creators connect their Stripe account via the Creator Dashboard. Payments are sent directly to their Stripe account via Stripe Connect destination charges. Payout timing follows Stripe's standard schedule (typically 2 business days).
What's the 2% commission used for?
Platform maintenance, server costs, CDN bandwidth, payment processing infrastructure, and continued development of Atlantist BIM. It's intentionally kept low so creators keep more of their earnings.
Are refunds available?
Buyers can request a refund within 7 days of purchase if the asset is defective or not as described. Creator payouts are adjusted accordingly. Contact support@atlantist.cloud for refund requests.
Can creators set their own prices?
Yes. Creators set their own listing price. Smart pricing provides a suggested minimum for sub-$5 assets to ensure margins remain healthy, but it's optional guidance — creators choose their own prices.
Do external assets (BIMObject, Poliigon, etc.) follow this model?
External assets are listed at the distributor's pricing and link out to the external platform. Atlantist does not process payments for external assets — no commission applies.
