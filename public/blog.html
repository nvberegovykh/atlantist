Simulation

Analysis Engines

Five specialized simulation engines — structural, energy, MEP, fire protection, geotechnical — all running in the browser or on the backend.

OpenSees structural analysis panel. Animated deflection and stress visualization in 3D. DCR (Demand-to-Capacity Ratio) color map. Code check PASS/FAIL per member.

Energy simulation with ASHRAE JS engine. 8,760-hour annual timeline player. EN-001 and EN-008 NYC compliance. EN-1 Workbook tab exports .xlsm with actual geometry baked in.

MEP simulation across three disciplines. HVAC runner with VAV terminals and duct routing. Plumbing fixture counts. Electrical panel sizing and load calculations.

Fire protection engine: NFPA 13 sprinkler, NFPA 101 egress, NFPA 92 smoke control, FDNY filing compliance. FDS smoke/temperature/pressure visualization modes.

Geotechnical engineering panel: bearing capacity (Terzaghi, Meyerhof, Hansen), elastic/consolidation settlement, lateral earth pressure, pile capacity, slope stability. OpenSees .tcl input generation.

Code Compliance Built In

IBC 1013/1014 (stairs and railings), ASHRAE 90.1 (energy), NFPA 13/101/92 (fire), NYC EN-001/008 (DOB filing), NAVFAC (geotechnical) — all at your fingertips without leaving the model.
