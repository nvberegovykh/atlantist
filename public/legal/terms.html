4A. Acceptable Use & Safety

Prohibition

Atlantist may not be used to design, model, document, or share any substance, structure, object, system, or assembly intended to cause harm to any person, property, or the environment.

Definition — Intention

A model is created with harmful intent when the user purposefully designs it with knowledge that its primary or foreseeable use is to cause harm. Intention is inferred from model content, descriptions, metadata, communications, and reasonable interpretation by the review team.

Definition — Harmful

Harmful means causing or being reasonably likely to cause physical injury, death, illegal activity, or material damage to persons, property, public safety, or the environment. This includes but is not limited to weapons, explosive devices, structures designed to fail, illicit substance synthesis apparatus, and surveillance or restraint equipment intended for non-consensual use.

Review Process

When a project is reported or flagged:

  1. Report received — the project is automatically and temporarily locked pending review.
  2. Human review — the Atlantist Trust & Safety team reviews the project, typically within 72 hours.
  3. Uphold — the project is permanently erased; the owner's account enters a 30-day grace export-only period, after which the account is suspended.
  4. Dismiss — the lock is removed; the owner receives a 50% discount on their next 3 billing cycles as compensation.

Right of Appeal

Users may appeal a Trust & Safety decision within 14 days of the decision by emailing support@atlantist.cloud with their account email, project details, and a description of the intended use.
