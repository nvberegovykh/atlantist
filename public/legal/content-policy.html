Content Policy

Atlantist is a browser-based Building Information Modeling platform for architectural and engineering design. This policy governs what users may model, simulate, or store on the platform.

⚠️ Core principle: Atlantist may not be used to model, design, or simulate any structure, object, or substance intended to cause harm to persons, animals, property, or any living or inanimate thing.

1. Prohibited Uses — Tier 1 (Immediate Account Termination)

The following categories of content are strictly prohibited. Accounts found in violation are terminated immediately, all models are deleted without notice, and no refund is issued:

  • Weapons of mass destruction — nuclear, radiological, biological, chemical weapons or delivery systems
  • Lethal weaponry and military hardware — firearms, artillery, missiles, military-grade explosives, cluster munitions
  • Combat blades, throwing weapons configured for attack or stealth — knives designed for combat deployment, weighted throwing knives, spring-loaded or ballistic blades
  • Explosive devices and improvised explosive devices (IEDs) — any model whose geometry or stated purpose matches the construction of a functioning explosive
  • Any object whose geometric proportions, material specifications, scale, or stated purpose indicate primary use for injury, death, or property damage

Tier 1 violations are assessed by qualified architectural and engineering review. Stated user intent is not a defense when Tier 1 criteria are clearly met based on objective geometric, material, or scale indicators.

2. Prohibited Uses — Tier 2 (30-Day Termination)

The following may result in account suspension pending review:

  • Structures or objects that are ambiguous in intent but exhibit Tier 1 characteristics
  • Models with metadata, tags, or descriptions that explicitly reference harmful use cases
  • Content that circumvents automated scanning (e.g., encrypted geometry, split models designed to avoid flagging)

3. Term Definitions

"Harmful"

"Harmful" means objects, structures, or substances whose design, material specification, or functional configuration is primarily intended to cause physical injury, death, or property damage to persons, animals, or property.

"Intended" / "Intention"

"Intended" or "Intention" means the reasonably inferred purpose of the model based on its geometric proportions, material specifications, scale, metadata, tags, or stated use case — as assessed by qualified architectural or engineering review. Stated user intent is not a defense when Tier 1 criteria are clearly met.

4. Automated Scanning

Atlantist employs automated content-scanning systems to flag models that exhibit characteristics consistent with Tier 1 or Tier 2 violations. Scanning analyzes:

  • Geometric proportions and scale ratios
  • Material specifications (explosive compounds, high-strength alloys, reactive materials)
  • Structural configurations (muzzle devices, explosive housings, fragmentation patterns)
  • Model metadata, tags, and naming conventions
  • User-provided descriptions and annotations

Automated scanning is a best-effort measure. Some legitimate architectural content (e.g., gun ranges, military base models) may be flagged for human review. False positives are handled via the appeal process.

6. Appeal Process

If you believe your model was incorrectly flagged, you may submit an appeal:

  • Email: content-review@atlantist.cloud
  • Deadline: Within 7 days of the review decision
  • Required information: Account email, model ID, description of intended architectural/engineering use, any supporting documentation

Appeals are reviewed by a separate team from the initial decision-maker. We aim to resolve appeals within 14 days. You will receive written notification of the outcome.

7. False Flag Compensation

If your appeal is successful (model cleared as legitimate architectural content with no violation), you will receive a 50% credit on your next 3 billing cycles. This credit is applied automatically and does not require a separate request.

9. Model Deletion

Flagged models confirmed as Tier 1 violations are erased immediately upon confirmation, without waiting for the 30-day grace period. All other models in the account receive the standard grace period.

11. Reporting Abuse

If you believe someone is using Atlantist in violation of this policy, report it immediately at abuse@atlantist.cloud. Include a description of the content and, if possible, the model identifier. Reports may be submitted anonymously; we investigate all submissions.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this policy to reflect new prohibited categories or improved detection methods. Material changes take effect 30 days after notice to registered users. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the current version.
