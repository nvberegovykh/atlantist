Acceptable Use & Safety

  • Atlantist is for lawful architectural and engineering work only. You may not use it to design, model, document, or share any substance, structure, object, system, or assembly intended to cause harm to any person, property, or the environment.
  • Prohibited content includes but is not limited to: weapons, explosive devices, structures designed to fail, illicit substance synthesis apparatus, and surveillance or restraint equipment intended for non-consensual use.
  • Intent is inferred from model content, descriptions, metadata, and communications. "Hypothetical" framing does not exempt a model from policy review.

What happens if a project is reported

1Automatic lock — the project is immediately locked pending review.
2Human review — the Atlantist Trust & Safety team reviews within 72 hours.
3Uphold — project is permanently erased; account enters a 30-day export-only grace period, then suspension.
4Dismiss — lock removed; you receive 50% off your next 3 billing cycles as compensation.

You may appeal any Trust & Safety decision within 14 days by emailing support@atlantist.cloud.
