Atlantist Contributor Agreement
Version 1.0  ·  Effective upon signature

Company: LIBER Creative LLC, a New York limited liability company ("Company")

Contributor: The individual or entity that signs this Agreement electronically ("Contributor")

This Atlantist Contributor Agreement ("Agreement") sets out the terms under which the Contributor participates in the Atlantist platform (atlantist.cloud) and earns a proportional share of net platform profits.

1. Nature of Relationship

This Agreement does not create an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture, or agency between the Contributor and the Company. Contributor is an independent contributor to a community-owned platform.

No minimum contribution is required. No salary, hourly rate, or guaranteed payment of any kind is owed. All compensation is entirely discretionary and prospective, as described in Section 4.

2. Eligible Contributions

The following activities are eligible for point awards under this Agreement:

  • Code commits merged into the main repository branch
  • Approved and merged pull requests
  • Closed issues (bug reports, feature requests) filed by the Contributor
  • Documentation contributions accepted by the Company
  • AI-generated contributions credited to a registered Contributor account (including contributions by AI agents such as Wallt)
  • Other contributions explicitly approved and categorized by a Company administrator

The Company — including its owner, employees, and AI agents operating under Company accounts — also earns points through the same system on equal terms.

3. Copyright Assignment & Attribution

By submitting a Contribution, the Contributor irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to the Contribution, including all copyrights and other intellectual property rights worldwide, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

In exchange, the Company will maintain publicly visible attribution to each Contributor (via GitHub commit history and the Atlantist leaderboard) for the duration of the platform's operation. Attribution is a courtesy and does not constitute a right of approval over how the Contribution is used.

4. Points & Profit Sharing

Upon approval and contract signature, the Contributor is enrolled in the Atlantist points system. Points are awarded automatically and/or manually according to the current point schedule maintained by the Company.

  1. Points are non-transferable and carry no voting rights, no governance rights, and no claim on Company assets outside of the profit-sharing mechanism described herein.
  2. Each Contributor's share percentage is calculated as:
    Contributor Points ÷ Total Approved Contributor Points × 100
  3. If the platform generates net profit in a given period, the Company shall distribute a portion of net profits (the "Distribution Pool") to approved contributors no less than annually, provided the platform is profitable.
  4. The Company retains sole discretion over the size of the Distribution Pool, timing, and whether any profit is declared available for distribution.
  5. The Company shall withhold all legally required taxes from distributions and remit them to the appropriate authorities. Contributor is responsible for their own tax obligations in their jurisdiction.
  6. Points may be deducted for code reverted due to quality issues, violations of the code of conduct, or other reasons determined by the Company at its sole discretion.

5. Stripe Connect & Payout

To receive profit distributions, the Contributor must connect a Stripe Express account via the platform dashboard. Payouts will be issued via Stripe's standard transfer mechanisms. The Company is not responsible for delays, failures, or fees imposed by Stripe or the Contributor's financial institution.

If a Contributor does not connect Stripe within 90 days of a declared distribution, their share for that period may be forfeited or held at the Company's discretion.

6. Contributor Status

Contributor accounts may have one of three statuses:

  • Pending — application submitted, awaiting review.
  • Approved — eligible to earn points and receive distributions.
  • Suspended — access to the points system is paused pending investigation or resolution.

The Company may change a Contributor's status at any time for any reason, including but not limited to violations of this Agreement, the platform's code of conduct, or applicable law.

7. Confidentiality

Contributor agrees not to disclose any non-public information about the Company's financials, distribution amounts, infrastructure, or business strategy that is shared with them in the course of their contributions. Leaderboard data (points, rankings) is public information.

8. Representations & Warranties

Contributor represents and warrants that:

  • They have the legal capacity to enter into this Agreement.
  • All Contributions are original works authored by them (or AI tools operating under their account) and do not infringe on the intellectual property rights of any third party.
  • The Contributor's GitHub account belongs to them and is not shared with unauthorized parties.

9. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Company's total liability to the Contributor under this Agreement shall not exceed the total distributions actually paid to the Contributor in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. In no event shall the Company be liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages.

10. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days' written notice to the other party. Upon termination, the Contributor's right to earn future points ceases; accrued points as of the effective termination date will be honored in the next scheduled distribution if one occurs.

The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately upon a material breach by the Contributor.

11. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, USA, without regard to its conflict of law provisions.

Any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) under its Commercial Arbitration Rules. The arbitration shall take place in New York, New York, or remotely if agreed by both parties. Judgment on the award may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, either party may seek injunctive relief in a court of competent jurisdiction to protect intellectual property rights.

12. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, understandings, or agreements. This Agreement may not be modified except by a written amendment signed by both parties or a new version published by the Company, which the Contributor must re-sign.

For questions about this Agreement, contact legal@atlantist.cloud.
