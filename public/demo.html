🏛 Architecture
Architecture
Exterior envelope (80′ × 120′ Manhattan footprint), curtain wall north/east, brick masonry south/west, interior partitions. 11 levels (ground + mezz + 8 typical + roof) with parametric level binding. 80 rooms, 2 stair cores.
80 rooms 10 floor plates 2 stairs DAG-solved
🔩 Structure
Structural Engineering
W14x90 steel columns on 5×7 grid, W18x35 spandrel beams at each floor, W24x76 transfer beams at podium level (3.658m). ASCE 7-22 code checks with pre-computed DCR stress values. 350 members, all PASS. Max DCR 0.89 — within limit.
350 members DCR ≤ 0.90 ASCE 7-22 AISC 360-22

Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing
VAV HVAC (40 zones, supply/return/exhaust risers, branch ducts + diffusers per floor). Wet-wall plumbing: cold/hot water, soil, vent stacks. NFPA 13 wet sprinkler: ring main + 240 heads. 400A MDP panels + EMT conduit + lighting/receptacle circuits.
40 VAV zones 240 sprinkler heads 4 plumbing risers 400A electrical
