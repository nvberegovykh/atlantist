How RECT works

01

Fixed costs split equally

Infrastructure, support, and compliance cost us $29/mo. Polsia charges us $29/mo for the platform. Both are shared equally across all users — not just paying customers.

02

Marginal cost scales down

API calls, storage, and compute cost us $0.20 per user per month. This is added to the formula: (C_fixed + $0.20×N) / N. At 10 users, each pays $6.00. At 1,000 users, each pays $5.26.

03

$5 floor — always

No user pays less than $5/mo. This protects dormant accounts from being subsidized unfairly and ensures we can keep the service running even for low-usage users.

04

No tiers, no premium version

Every user runs the same formula. No enterprise pricing, no pro add-ons, no feature gates that degrade the cooperative model. The graph above shows exactly what you'd pay at every network size.
