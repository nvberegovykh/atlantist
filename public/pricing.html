Questions

How is my actual bill calculated?+
Every day we track your real infrastructure usage across 5 categories: compute (simulation runs), storage (PostgreSQL bytes), bandwidth (file transfers + WebSocket), 3rd-party API calls, and hosting amortization. At month end, we sum it all and multiply by 2. Your bill is max($5, total × 2).
What's the $5 minimum floor?+
Even dormant accounts cost us money to keep running — the database is still there, the service is still up. So we charge a $5/mo minimum regardless of actual usage. Active users effectively pay $0 for hosting since it amortizes across their simulation and storage costs.
What happens if my usage is very high?+
If total monthly infrastructure cost across all users hits $150/mo, our system automatically triggers a cost optimization analysis. We identify the top cost driver and generate engineering tasks to reduce it by 5-10×. You'll be notified of any changes that affect your billing. You always have the right to cancel if the model doesn't work for you.
Can I see my real-time cost?+
Yes — sign in and visit your account dashboard. You'll see a live month-to-date cost breakdown by category (compute, storage, bandwidth, API, hosting) plus a predicted bill for next month based on your last 3 months of usage.
Is there a free trial?+
Yes — no credit card required to start. You'll only be charged if you choose to continue after the trial ends. During the trial you'll see your actual running cost in your account so there are no surprises.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes — cancel anytime from your account dashboard. Your data is held in read-only mode for 90 days before deletion. Reactivate anytime within that window at no extra cost.
Do you offer team or enterprise pricing?+
Team collaboration is on the roadmap. For enterprise needs (SSO, dedicated support, custom integrations), contact us at support@atlantist.cloud. Enterprise accounts get a dedicated cost breakdown and custom pricing negotiations based on actual usage patterns.
Who owns the models I create?+
You do. All project data belongs to you. We don't claim any rights to your work. See our Terms of Service for details.
